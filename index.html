The parentheses

Our mark and our method, taken from the dictionary's own structure: the small container that holds a definition apart from the sentence around it. Every project is pulled aside, defined, and given room to mean something before it's dropped back into the world.

Define

Start from the vision and the problem. Find what's actually true about the brand or artist.

Design and build

Concept, design and build loop until the idea holds up on screen and on set.

Release

The product goes out into the world, with the story attached.

Inner Circle for hire

The creative-led division of The 24th Vision, and the youth talent shaping its sharpest, most narrative-driven ideas. We don't set out to take a beautiful picture. We find the story underneath it, then build the picture around that. We analyse, we curate, we tell a story.
